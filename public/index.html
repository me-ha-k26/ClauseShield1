// KNOW THE LEGAL CONTEXT

Rental disputes need more than assumptions

A deduction is not automatically unlawful simply because a tenant disagrees with it. The rental agreement, evidence, applicable state law and circumstances all matter.

Indian Contract Act, 1872

Section 74 concerns reasonable compensation where a contract stipulates a sum payable for breach. Its application depends on the facts and contract.

Model Tenancy Act, 2021

The Model Tenancy Act is a model framework circulated to states and Union Territories. Its provisions should not be assumed to apply uniformly throughout India.

Evidence and agreement terms

Inspection records, photographs, invoices, inventory lists and written communication can help explain a deposit dispute.

Legal information is provided for awareness. Check the current law applicable to your location and consult a qualified advocate for legal advice.

// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions tenants often ask

What is ClauseShield?

ClauseShield is a proposed tenant-support platform designed to help users review rental documents, understand potential deposit deductions, organize evidence and prepare responses.

Can ClauseShield help with security deposit disputes?

It is designed to help organize deposit amounts, proposed deductions, supporting evidence and possible responses. It cannot guarantee a refund or determine the final legal outcome.

Does every landlord deduction count as illegal?

No. Whether a deduction is justified depends on the agreement, the condition of the property, supporting evidence and applicable law.

Does the Model Tenancy Act apply everywhere in India?

No. The Model Tenancy Act is a model framework circulated to states and Union Territories. Its adoption and applicable tenancy rules can differ by jurisdiction.

Does ClauseShield replace a lawyer?

No. ClauseShield is intended as an informational assistance tool. Complex disputes and legal decisions should be discussed with a qualified advocate.

Can I use photographs and messages as evidence?

You can organize relevant photographs, receipts and written communication. Their evidentiary value depends on authenticity, context and applicable law.
