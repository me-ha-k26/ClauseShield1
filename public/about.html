// ABOUT CLAUSESHIELD

Making rental rights easier to understand.

ClauseShield is a tenant-focused platform concept designed to help renters understand rental agreements, review property-related deductions and prepare clearer responses to rental disputes.

Explore lease audit

// OUR MISSION

Bringing clarity to the rental experience

Rental agreements and security-deposit deductions can be difficult to understand, especially when tenants are unfamiliar with legal terminology.

ClauseShield aims to make this process easier by organising important information, highlighting clauses that may need attention and helping tenants prepare informed questions or responses.
