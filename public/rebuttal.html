Deposit Deduction Dispute

Draft response

SAMPLE

Dear Landlord,

I am writing regarding the proposed deduction from my security deposit for repainting charges.

I would appreciate an itemised explanation of the deduction, along with supporting invoices or evidence showing the actual cost incurred.

If the charges relate to ordinary deterioration from normal residential use, I request that these be distinguished from damage beyond normal wear and tear.

I would be grateful if you could review the deduction and share the relevant documentation so that we can resolve this matter fairly.

Regards,
[Your Name]

This is an illustrative sample, not a generated legal response. The final draft should reflect your actual agreement, evidence and applicable law.
