These are illustrative examples. The classification depends on the severity, cause, rental agreement and available evidence.

What is the difference?

Ordinary wear & tear

  • Natural paint fading
  • Minor ageing of fixtures
  • Light marks from everyday use
  • Normal deterioration over time

Possible tenant damage

  • Broken fixtures due to misuse
  • Large holes or severe wall damage
  • Damage beyond normal ageing
  • Repair needs supported by evidence

Important information

These examples are general illustrations, not automatic legal classifications. Property condition, the rental agreement, photographs, inspection records, applicable state law and the cause of damage must all be considered before deciding whether a deduction is justified.
